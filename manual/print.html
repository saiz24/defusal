The Expert holds this manual. The Defuser holds the device.

The Defuser may not read this manual. You may not look at the device. Modules are not named on the device — find yours by what the Defuser describes to you.

Before you start

  1. Agree who is the Defuser and who is the Expert.
  2. The Expert reads a module's section aloud; the Defuser reads the device aloud.
  3. Three mistakes and the device goes off. So does the clock running out.
